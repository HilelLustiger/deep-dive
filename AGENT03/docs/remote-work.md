# Remote work

You can work from home up to 3 days a week. Teams agree among themselves which days they spend in the office, so that people who work together are there on the same days.

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Thursday. During core hours, be reachable for meetings and messages. Outside them, plan your day as you like. Fridays have no core hours.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days a year, because of tax rules. Tell the people team at least a month before you go.

The office in Haifa is open from 7:00 to 21:00. Lunch is provided on Tuesdays and Thursdays, the most common office days.
