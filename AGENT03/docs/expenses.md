# Expenses and travel

Kestrel Labs pays for reasonable costs of work travel. Submit every expense in Ledgerly with a photo of the receipt within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses submitted later are not paid.

Flights are booked in economy class. For a single flight longer than 6 hours, you may book premium economy. Business class needs approval from a director, in writing, before booking.

While traveling, meals are covered up to 45 euros per day. Alcohol is not covered. Hotels should cost no more than 150 euros per night in most cities; in London, Paris and New York the limit is 220 euros.

Taxis are covered between the airport and the hotel, and late at night. For other trips, use public transport where it's practical. A rental car needs approval from your team lead.

Conference tickets are paid from your team's learning budget of 1,000 euros per person per year. Ask your team lead before buying one.
