# Your first weeks

Before your first day, you get an email with your accounts: email, chat, TimeBoard for time off, and Ledgerly for expenses. Set up two-factor authentication on all of them on day one.

Every new employee gets a buddy, someone from another team who has been at Kestrel Labs for at least a year. Your buddy meets you on your first day and checks in every week for your first month.

Security training is required, and must be finished within 10 days of your start date. Until it is, you won't get access to customer data.

At the end of your first month, you meet your team lead to talk about how it's going. At the end of your third month, you have a longer review, and your probation period ends.
