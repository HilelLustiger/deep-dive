# Vacation and time off

Every full-time employee at Kestrel Labs gets 24 vacation days a year. Part-time employees get vacation days in proportion to their hours: someone working three days a week gets 15 days.

Vacation days are added at the start of each calendar year. You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, or they are lost. Days beyond those 5 are lost on January 1.

Requests for more than 3 days in a row must be made in TimeBoard at least two weeks in advance, and approved by your team lead. Shorter requests need only a few days' notice.

Sick days are separate from vacation days and are not limited. For an absence of more than 3 days in a row, send a doctor's note to people@kestrel.example.

Public holidays are days off for everyone and don't count as vacation days. The list for the coming year is published in TimeBoard every December.
