# Equipment

On your first day you choose a laptop: a MacBook Pro 14 or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are replaced every 3 years, or earlier if one breaks and can't be repaired.

For your desk at home, you can spend up to 400 euros on a monitor and up to 150 euros on a keyboard, mouse and headset together. Order them through the IT portal so they arrive already registered.

If your laptop is lost or stolen, report it to it@kestrel.example within 24 hours, so it can be locked remotely. You won't be charged for a lost laptop, but late reports put customer data at risk.

Equipment belongs to Kestrel Labs. When you leave, return your laptop and any monitor within 14 days. Keyboards, mice and headsets are yours to keep.
